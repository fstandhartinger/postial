# Sources for `/compare/*`

Retrieved 2026-09-13 from the vendors’ public pages. This is the evidence register for every statement about Hootsuite, Postiz, Buffer, Planable, Sendible or Sprout Social rendered by the comparison pages. “Not confirmed” means the checked public source did not establish the capability; it is not a claim that the vendor can never offer it.

## Hootsuite

- Pricing, plans, billing, account limits, trial and team approvals: [Hootsuite plans](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans) — retrieved 2026-09-13. It lists Standard from $99, Professional from $199 and Advanced up to $399 per user/month on annual billing; says prices exclude taxes; describes 10 versus unlimited social accounts; says trials are 14 days with no card; and lists team approvals on Advanced.
- Network catalogue and platform-specific claims: [Hootsuite integrations](https://www.hootsuite.com/platform/integrations) — retrieved 2026-09-13. It publicly lists Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest and Bluesky, and says Threads publishing/scheduling/drafting is included on all plans. The page’s FAQ says some Instagram features need an Instagram Business account.
- Approval detail: [Hootsuite Advanced](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans/advanced) — retrieved 2026-09-13. It describes approval workflows, internal collaboration and message routing on Advanced. Neither this page nor the pricing page establishes a login-free external reviewer link, so the comparison says that flow is not confirmed.
- Failure handling: [Hootsuite publishing error guidance](https://help.hootsuite.com/s/article/troubleshoot-errors) — retrieved 2026-09-13. The comparison does not claim a Hootsuite retry control; it records that a per-post retry workflow was not verified from the checked public material.

## Postiz

- Cloud pricing, channel/team limits, trial and self-hosting: [Postiz pricing](https://postiz.com/pricing) — retrieved 2026-09-13. It lists Standard $29/month, Team $39/month, Pro $49/month and Ultimate $99/month, with their channel/team allowances; says the cloud trial is 7 days; and says Postiz offers an open-source product that can be deployed on a customer-owned cloud/server.
- Agency grouping and team roles: [Postiz homepage FAQ](https://postiz.com/) — retrieved 2026-09-13. It describes customer groups on Team, Pro and Ultimate, unlimited team members on those plans, and Admin/Member roles. It does not establish a login-free external approval link.
- API, n8n, failure semantics and supported platforms: [Postiz API overview](https://docs.postiz.com/public-api/introduction) — retrieved 2026-09-13. It documents a public API and custom n8n node, HTTP error statuses including retry-with-exponential-backoff for 5xx, and platforms including X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Mastodon, Bluesky and Telegram. The comparison now states only that Postiz documents these platforms; it does not infer a Postial-network overlap or turn API error handling into a claim of a visible per-post UI retry button.

## Buffer

- Per-channel pricing, plan limits, API quotas, approval workflows, trial and supported channels: [Buffer pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing) — retrieved 2026-09-13 (HTTP 200; robots.txt allows `/pricing`). It lists a free plan for up to 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts per channel; Essentials at $5 per channel/month and Team at $10 per channel/month on annual billing (yearly toggle, “save 2 months”); Team includes unlimited team members, access levels and content approval workflows described as managing who can draft and approve posts across the team; API access is on every plan (Free 1 key/3,000 requests per 30 days, Team 5 keys/15,000 per 30 days); n8n is listed among integrations; and the channel table lists Bluesky, Mastodon, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest and Google Business Profile with per-channel capability differences. The page shows 14-day trial calls to action; it does not state a card requirement, a hosting region, failure/retry behaviour, or tax handling, and the comparison says so.

## Planable

- Per-workspace pricing, plan limits, platforms, API and trial: [Planable pricing](https://planable.io/pricing/) — retrieved 2026-09-13 (HTTP 200; robots.txt allows `/pricing/`). It lists Basic at $33 per workspace/month (60 posts, unlimited users, 4 social pages per workspace) and Pro at $49 per workspace/month (150 posts, 10 social pages, public API); prices are in USD and exclude taxes; it recommends one workspace per client and offers a volume discount for 5+ workspaces; the trial is the first 50 posts free with no time limit and no credit card, excluding X publishing; and the FAQ lists scheduling for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Google Business Profile and Threads. The pricing FAQ states GDPR compliance but no hosting region.

## Sendible

- Plan pricing, workspaces, approvals, integrations, trial and supported networks: [Sendible pricing](https://www.sendible.com/pricing) — retrieved 2026-09-13 (HTTP 200; robots.txt does not disallow `/pricing`). The plan figures are embedded in the served HTML as price attributes: Core $35, Plus $99, Premium $199, Elite $299 and Enterprise $750 per month billed monthly, with annual billing 15% lower ($29.75/$84.15/$169.15/$254.15/$637.50 per month); the page shows US Dollar, Euro and Pound prices. Plans include 1, 3, 7, 15 and 50 workspaces respectively, with 6 social profiles allocated per workspace, and unlimited users on every plan (“team members, freelancers, stakeholders, and clients”). Content approval workflows are listed from Plus: “Assign tasks to teammates or clients through content approval workflows before publishing” — whether clients approve without a Sendible login is not established, and the comparison says so. The page lists a Zapier integration, cloud integrations (Google Drive, Dropbox) and automations from Airtable, Notion, Monday.com and ClickUp, but no public API or n8n node. Its supported-networks section documents publishing to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Google My Business, YouTube, WordPress, Threads and Bluesky; Mastodon and Telegram are not in the listed set. The page advertises a 14-day trial with “No card required”, and the FAQ says the card is requested at the end of the trial and billed automatically if added during it; a daily posting limit of 100 posts per user applies during the trial. It does not state a hosting region or tax handling, and it does not describe a per-post publishing error and retry workflow; the comparison says so.

## Sprout Social

- Per-seat pricing, plans, approvals, API and trial: [Sprout Social pricing](https://sproutsocial.com/pricing/) — retrieved 2026-09-13 (HTTP 200; robots.txt has no `Disallow` for `/pricing/`). It lists Essentials at $79 per seat/month on annual billing ($99 billed monthly) with up to 5 social profiles, and Standard $199, Professional $299 and Advanced $399 per seat/month under “Annual billing”; monthly rates for Standard and up are not stated. The plan overview lists 5, 5 and unlimited social profiles for Essentials/Standard, Professional and Advanced. The embedded plan table includes an external approval workflow on Advanced and Enterprise only — external stakeholders “can review social posts before they’re published without needing to log in to Sprout” — internal message approval workflows from Professional, and the Sprout API (with helpdesk and BI integrations) from Advanced. The page advertises a 30-day trial with “No credit card required.” It does not state a hosting region, tax handling, an n8n node, or a per-post publishing error and retry workflow; the comparison says so.
- Network catalogue: [Sprout Social integrations](https://sproutsocial.com/integrations/) — retrieved 2026-09-13 (HTTP 200). It lists Bluesky, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Reddit, Snapchat, Threads, TikTok, X and YouTube as social networks, plus messaging channels such as Facebook Messenger; Mastodon and Telegram are not in the listed set.
