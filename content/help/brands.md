# Create a brand

A brand groups one client’s channels and sets the timezone for its posts.

## Steps

1. Open [Brands](/app/brands) and create a brand.
2. Enter its name, timezone and visual details using the form.
3. Choose the timezone where you plan the client’s publishing schedule, for example Europe/Berlin.
4. Open the brand and use Connect a channel.
5. Select this brand when composing or bulk planning.

Starter allows three brands; Agency allows fifteen. After a downgrade, the oldest brands within the limit stay editable and extra brands become read-only. Dates are interpreted in the brand timezone; ambiguous or nonexistent daylight-saving minutes must be changed.
