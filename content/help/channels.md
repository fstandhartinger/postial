# Connect and maintain channels

Connect Bluesky, Mastodon or Telegram and review connection health across your brands.

## Steps

1. Open [Brands](/app/brands), select a brand and find Connect a channel.
2. Follow the guide for Bluesky, Mastodon or Telegram below; enter only the credentials that guide requests.
3. Confirm that the displayed account or channel belongs to the intended client.
4. Open [Channels](/app/channels) to see connection health, the latest error and the last successful post.
5. Use Reconnect when authorization expires; connect the same account to update its existing connection.
6. Use the manual check when diagnosing a problem, at most once a minute. Automatic checks run daily.

Disconnect stops future publishing through that connection and removes its active credentials; it does not remove already published posts. X, Threads, LinkedIn and Instagram/Facebook are in preparation, subject to platform approval with no confirmed launch date. Check [Roadmap](/roadmap) for availability and the optional network waitlist.
