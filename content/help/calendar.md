# Use the calendar

Review the month or week and adjust unstarted posts without changing their content.

## Steps

1. Open [Calendar](/app/calendar) and choose the month or week view.
2. Dates use each brand’s timezone; confirm the timezone on its Brand page.
3. Open a post to inspect its status, content and scheduled time.
4. Use Reschedule on an unstarted scheduled or approved post and select a future time.
5. Use Plan several posts for a campaign with multiple dates.

Rescheduling only the date preserves approval. Saving content edits resets approval. A scheduled date is not a delivery guarantee: approvals, subscription access and network errors can hold publication.
