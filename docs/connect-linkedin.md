# Connect LinkedIn

In **Brands → your brand → Connect a channel**, choose **Connect LinkedIn**.
Approve Postial in LinkedIn and return to the app; your personal profile will
appear as an active channel. Postial never asks for your LinkedIn password.

LinkedIn posts support up to 3,000 characters. This adapter currently publishes
text-only posts; image attachments are omitted with a warning. Company pages are
not supported yet and require a separate LinkedIn API review.

If access expires, connect the same profile again. A rate limit or provider error
is retried by Postial; an uncertain result should be checked on LinkedIn
before manually retrying.
